Financial services

One framework, many models.

GuardX works with large companies, especially in financial services. The evidence already in the firm becomes one risk picture, and review moves only when that risk does.

The framework

Centralized, but agile.

Risk is read from evidence the firm already holds, then sent only where it matters.

  1. 01

    One picture

    Data, access, models and controls already sit in the firm. GuardX reads them into one risk picture. A missing record is named as a gap, not guessed.

  2. 02

    The right desk

    The decision goes only to the functions the risk implicates. Everyone else stays off the path, and the reason is written down.

  3. 03

    Only what moved

    A new release is compared with the last approval. Review reopens only where the risk actually changed.

With the firm

Governance that keeps pace with the models.

Large institutions run many systems and answer for all of them. GuardX holds that answer in one place, without freezing the work.
