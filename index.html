The assessment

From regulation text to a finding you can defend.

Each regulation is broken into testable requirements. Each requirement is checked against your model's evidence, and every result cites the clause it answers to.

01

Scope

Classify the model by use, jurisdiction and risk tier, and establish which regimes apply before any testing starts.

02

Map

Turn each applicable regulation into specific, testable requirements, reconciled where regimes overlap so nothing is assessed twice.

03

Evidence

Draw on what the firm already holds: model documentation, validation reports, data lineage, access and monitoring records. Missing records are flagged as gaps, never assumed.

04

Findings

A result for every requirement — met, gap or evidence missing — with the clause cited, an accountable owner and the remediation needed.

What you get

Ready for your validators, your auditors and your supervisor.

Outputs are built to be handed over, not reworked. Every conclusion traces back to a clause and a piece of evidence.

Requirement-level findings

A matrix of every requirement by model, with status, cited clause and supporting evidence.

Gap register

Each gap with an accountable owner, the remediation required and the regulation it closes against.

Evidence pack

The records behind each finding, assembled for validation, internal audit or a supervisory request.

Re-assessment on change

When a model or a rule changes, only the affected requirements are re-tested.

GuardX Frontier Index · Free · Published quarterly

Which frontier models know the rules — and follow them?

Every quarter, GuardX ranks leading frontier models against the regulations financial institutions operate under. Each model is scored on two things: whether it understands a regulation, and whether it adheres to it while carrying out real operational work. The rankings and methodology are free and open to everyone.

Regulatory understanding

Can the model state what a rule requires, when it applies and where its limits are? Tested with questions grounded in regulation text and supervisory guidance.

Operational adherence

Does the model follow the rule while doing the work? Tested on realistic tasks — KYC reviews, adverse action notices, complaint handling, credit memos — and scored on whether the output is compliant.
